Outside of research, I love classical music, especially instrumental music. I haven't explored much vocal repertoire yet.

- **Beethoven:** I especially love the [32 piano sonatas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beethoven%27s_piano_sonatas) by [Ludwig van Beethoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven).
- **Brahms:** I love everything [Johannes Brahms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Brahms) wrote.
- **Favorite pianist:** [Sviatoslav Richter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sviatoslav_Richter)
- **Favorite conductor:** [Claudio Abbado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Abbado)
