I grew up in Beijing. Outside the lab, I like to stay active and spend a lot of time with music.

- **Sports:** swimming, jogging, cycling, and skiing
- **Instrument:** I play the [tuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuba)

## Listening

I mostly listen to classical and instrumental music.

- **Beethoven:** I keep coming back to his [32 piano sonatas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beethoven%27s_piano_sonatas), and above all [the last three, Op. 109, 110, and 111](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Qj411X7Kg/).
- **Brahms:** I love everything [Johannes Brahms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Brahms) wrote.
- **Favorite pianist:** [Sviatoslav Richter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sviatoslav_Richter)
- **Favorite conductor:** [Claudio Abbado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudio_Abbado)

### On repeat this year

- [Schumann, Fantasie in C major, Op. 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasie_(Schumann))
- [Hua'er (花儿乐队)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hua%27er)
- [He Yong (何勇), *Drum Tower* (钟鼓楼)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1q5411s7Wu/)
- 163braces
